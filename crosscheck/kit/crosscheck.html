Safety lens. Objective catalogue reconstructed from public predecessors of ED-153 (EUROCONTROL Recommendations for ANS Software, 2005), the Czech CAA ED-153 guide and EASA AMC/GM to Regulation (EU) 2017/373. Security lens. Requirements are the OWASP Application Security Verification Standard 5.0.0, © OWASP Foundation, licensed CC BY-SA 4.0, reproduced unmodified from github.com/OWASP/ASVS. A level is met only when every requirement at or below it passes.

AI lens. ISO/IEC 42001:2023 is cited by clause and control number with paraphrased titles only; the standard is © ISO/IEC. Confirm against a purchased copy. Clauses 4–10 are requirements of the management system and cannot be excluded; an Annex A control can be excluded only with a justification in the statement of applicability.

Status scale after Automotive SPICE; safety finding severity after EASA AMC 20-189; review stages after FAA Order 8110.49. Cross-check themes are CrossCheck's own mapping, not part of any of the three standards.
